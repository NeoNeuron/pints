Materials from earlier PINTS meetings. Each edition kept its own website, so this
page is an index rather than a mirror — follow the links for full programmes and
speaker lists.

## PINTS 2025

**5 December 2025** — Daniel Cohen amphitheatre, ENS Campus Jourdan, Paris.
Keynote by Julijana Gjorgjieva (Technical University of Munich).

[pints2025.sciencesconf.org](https://pints2025.sciencesconf.org) — programme,
speakers, and the registered-participant list.

## PINTS 2024

Keynote by Christian Machens (Champalimaud Foundation) on "Computing with spikes:
a geometric approach to spiking neural networks".

[pints.sciencesconf.org](https://pints.sciencesconf.org) — speakers, abstract
submission, registered participants, and poster information.

The site states neither the 2024 date nor the venue. It has also since been
edited to point visitors towards later editions, so parts of it now describe 2025
rather than 2024.

## PINTS 2022

**2 June 2022** — Jean-Jaurès auditorium, École Normale Supérieure, 29 rue d'Ulm,
Paris. Keynote by Justine Cassell (PRAIRIE and Carnegie Mellon) on "Sociality in
language and thought", alongside twelve invited speakers.

[p1nts.github.io](https://p1nts.github.io) — full programme with session times,
and the speaker list with affiliations. No abstracts, posters or photographs are
archived.
